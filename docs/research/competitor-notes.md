# Competitor Notes — Sammatin tila

## Local Farm Shops (Lähiruoka) — Direct Comparison

### Maatila Tahlo (Mutala, Ylöjärvi)
- **URL**: https://maatilatahlo.fi
- **Location**: Mutala, Ylöjärvi
- **Products**: Naudan jauheliha, paistit, fileet
- **Pricing**: 18 €/kg jauheliha, 56 €/kg fileepihvi, 28 €/kg paisti
- **Model**: Pre-order based, pickup from farm
- **Website**: Modern, clean, Joomla-based, has online store presence
- **Takeaway**: Direct competitor focused purely on beef; has clearer pricing

### Arvilan tila (Torppi)
- **URL**: http://www.arvilantila.fi
- **Products**: Luomunautaa (organic beef), broader product range
- **Model**: Has online store, broader selection
- **Takeaway**: More diversified, organic premium positioning

### Perkkión tila
- **Products**: Farm-direct beef
- **Takeaway**: Standard farm-direct model, less digital presence

### Tenhon tila
- **Products**: Farm-direct beef
- **Takeaway**: Similar model, no distinct digital differentiator

## Cabin Rentals — Comparison

### Lomarengas
- **Market**: Dominant Finnish cabin rental marketplace
- **Strength**: Massive inventory, booking engine, trust
- **Weakness**: Generic presentation, no personal farm experience

### eMökki
- **Model**: Finnish cabin rental aggregator
- **Strength**: Wide reach
- **Takeaway**: Listed on both Lomarengas and eMökki

### Airbnb
- **Market**: Global, cottages in Tampere region
- **Strength**: International reach, trusted booking
- **Weakness**: Variable quality, not farm-specific

## Sammatin tila's Unique Position

| Factor | Sammatin tila | Competitors |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Dairy + Beef + Cabins** | Unique triple offering | Most do 1-2 |
| **24/7 self-service** | Always open, no staffing | Most have limited hours |
| **Robotic milking** | Modern, visible | Varies |
| **Cabin location** | Näsijärvi shore, 25 km from Tampere | Closest to major city |
| **Self-service shop model** | Trust-based, minimal overhead | Few competitors do this |

## Design & UX Benchmarks for Redesign

### Good Examples
- Maatila Tahlo — Clean, modern, clear product categories
- Herkun maatila — Warm, inviting, good photo quality
- Visit Tampere official site — Professional tourism standard

### What to Avoid
- Heavy CMS platforms (Joomla, Wordpress) with slow load times
- Generic templates that hide farm personality
- Missing or outdated pricing/availability
- Non-responsive layouts
