# Cabin Details — Sammatin tila

## Overview

Three log/cottage cabins on a quiet lake shore, ~25 km northwest of Tampere. Available for summer season rental. The cabins are nestled in forest terrain on the Rajala family's farm property.

## Location Benefits

- **25–30 km from Tampere** (20–25 min drive)
- **20 min to Särkänniemi** amusement park
- **30 min to Pirkkahalli** (exhibition center, trade fairs)
- Close to nature: berry picking areas near Villakko
- Quiet rural roads for cycling
