# Business Analysis — Sammatin tila

## Business Model

Sammatin tila is a multi-stream agricultural business:

| Stream | Description | Revenue Contribution |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| Dairy (primary) | Milk production via robotic milking, sold to dairy | Major |
| Beef sales (secondary) | Direct sales from own herd via self-service shop | Moderate |
| Cabin rentals (secondary) | 3 seasonal cabins, summer only | Moderate |
| Animal place rentals | Rents barn space to other producers | Minor |
| Future: Ylinen development | Solar, data centers, daycare | Future |

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Local Farm Shops)
- **Maatila Tahlo** (Ylöjärvi, Mutala): Also sells beef mince, fillets, roasts. Pricing: mince 18 €/kg, fillet steaks 56 €/kg, sirloin roast 28 €/kg
- **Arvilan tila** (Torppi): Organic beef, online store, broader product range
- **Perkkión tila**: Farm-direct beef sales
- **Tenhon tila**: Farm-direct beef sales

### Differentiators
1. **24/7 self-service shop** — Always open, no staff needed for purchases
2. **Robotic milking** — Modern, efficient dairy operation
3. **Cabin rentals** — Unique multi-offering (farm stays + beach cabins)
4. **Location** — Only 25–30 min from Tampere city center
5. **Proximity to attractions** — 20 min to Särkänniemi, 30 min to Pirkkahalli

### Cabin Competitors
- Lomarengas listings in Sammatti area
- eMökki listings
- Individual cabin rentals on Näsijärvi shore
- Airbnb hosts in the region

## SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- Established farm with 20+ year track record under current owner
- Diversified revenue streams (milk, beef, cabins, rentals)
- Modern free-stall barn with robotic milking
- Strong local reputation (Young Entrepreneur award)
- 24/7 self-service shop with no staffing overhead
- Proximity to Tampere (3rd largest city in Finland)
- Näsijärvi lakefront cabin locations

### Weaknesses
- Current website very outdated (2009, static HTML)
- No functional online store
- No functional multi-language content
- No social media integration
- Limited digital presence and SEO
- Cabin season limited to summer
- No pricing information publicly available

### Opportunities
- Complete website overhaul with modern design
- SEO optimization for local search (Tampere region tourism)
- Social media marketing (Facebook 1,200+ followers, Instagram)
- Ylinen development (solar, data center, daycare)
- Partnership with Visit Tampere / regional tourism
- Cross-selling (shop + cabin packages)
- Email newsletter for beef product availability

### Threats
- Declining agricultural profitability in Finland
- Increasing regulation on dairy farming
- Competition from other farm shops
- Airbnb/booking platform dominance in cabin rentals
- Economic downturns affecting tourism
- Ylinen development risks (contamination, building permits, financing)

## Target Audiences for Website

1. **Local consumers** (Ylöjärvi, Tampere, Pirkanmaa) — Beef shop customers
2. **Tourists** (Finnish & international) — Cabin renters
3. **Day trippers** from Tampere region — Short getaways
4. **Business travelers** visiting Pirkkahalli/Tampere — Accommodation
5. **Families** — Child-friendly cabin amenities
6. **Anglers & nature lovers** — Lakeside cabins, fishing
7. **Other producers** — Animal place rentals

## Business Goals for Website

1. Present the farm story and operations transparently
2. Drive foot traffic to the 24/7 self-service shop
3. Generate cabin booking inquiries
4. Build brand trust through modern, credible web presence
5. Provide clear contact and location information
6. Support future growth (Ylinen development, new services)
