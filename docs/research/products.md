# Products — Sammatin tila

## 1. Dairy (Primary Production)

The main product of Sammatin tila is **milk**, produced by a herd of ~100+ cows. Key details:

- **Breeds**: Ayrshire (brown-white), Holstein-Friesian (black-white), Western Finnish cattle (länsisuomenkarja), kyyttö, crossbreeds (Ay-Fr, Jersey-Ay)
- **Milk production (2018)**: 9,004 L/cow/year
  - Fat: 4.15%
  - Protein: 3.35%
  - Somatic cell count: 219
- **Feeding**: Grass silage (timothy, meadow fescue, tall fescue, cocksfoot, red clover, reed canarygrass for young animals), small amount of corn, plus oats, wheat, and horse beans from neighboring farms. Robot feeding concentrate, minerals, and calf feed purchased externally.
- **Milk buyer**: Standard dairy processor (meijeri)
- **Calf feeding**: 8 L of milk/day for 8 weeks via bottle feeder → automatic feeder

## 2. Beef Products (Self-Service Shop)

Own beef from the farm's herd, sold through the 24/7 self-service shop. Product categories confirmed from the website and Facebook:

- **Jauheliha** (Minced meat) — Ground beef from own cattle
- **Paistit** (Roasts) — Various roast cuts
- **Fileet** (Fillets) — Fillet cuts

The shop operates on a trust-based system: customers enter, pick products, and pay (likely via mobile payment or cash box). The shop is always open.

The self-service shop also sells milk.

**Note**: The online store (verkkokauppa) link on the current site returns a 404 error. It has been discontinued. All beef sales are through the physical shop only.

## 3. Cabin Rentals

Three cabins available for summer season rental on the shores of a quiet lake near Tampere.

- **Capacity**: 6 persons max (3+3 sleeping places)
- **Type**: Log cabin (hirsimökki), 45+15 m², built 1993
- **Layout**: Open-plan kitchen-living room with sofa bed, 2 bedrooms (1 double bed, 1 bunk bed), dressing room, sauna with shower, terrace
- **Water**: Drilled well (cold + hot running water)
- **Amenities**: Electric stove, fridge with freezer, microwave, coffee maker, toaster, gas grill, radio, antenna for TV
- **Heating**: Electric, heat-storing fireplace, wood-fired sauna stove
- **Extras**: Bicycles, life vests, child safety items (crib, high chair, life vests 10-40kg on request), chemical WC on request
- **Distance**: 25 m to shore, 50 m to neighbor cabin
- **Distance to Tampere**: 25 km (20 min to Särkänniemi)
- **Restrictions**: No pets, no smoking indoors, no open fires, no tent/caravan without owner permission
- **Cleaning fee**: 60 € (on request at booking)
