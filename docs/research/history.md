# History — Sammatin tila

## Origins: The Sammatti Torppa

Sammatin tila traces its origins to "Sammatin torppa" (Sammatti's croft), a small tenant farm in the Takamaa area of Ylöjärvi. The exact founding date of the original croft is unknown, but it represents generations of farming tradition in the region.

## Modern Era

### 2003
The current owner, Suvi Rajala, took over the farm at age 20. She continued operations under the name "Sammatin tila". The farm had approximately 15 cows at this time.

### 2007
A new free-stall barn (pihatto) was completed, replacing older facilities. This modern barn allows cows to move freely between feeding, resting, and milking areas.

### 1997 (pre-takeover)
The young animal barn (nuorkarjapihatto) was built, which was already in place when Suvi took over.

### 2010s — Growth and Modernization
- Robotic milking system installed (year uncertain)
- Herd size grew significantly
- Revenue increased nearly 10x compared to startup
- Online booking system for cabins introduced (existing PHP calendar app)

### 2016 — Incorporation
As the business expanded, operations were converted to a limited company (osakeyhtiö): **Sammatin tila Oy**. This provided legal separation of business and personal assets, clearer governance, and tax advantages.

### 2016 — Young Entrepreneur Award
Suvi Rajala was awarded "Nuori Yrittäjä 2016" (Young Entrepreneur of the Year) by Ylöjärven Yrittäjät. The award recognized her persistent, goal-oriented work in developing the family farm. She was described as running the farm for 13 years already at age 33.

### 2024–2025 — Ylinen Rehabilitation Centre Acquisition
The former Ylinen rehabilitation centre (a developmental disabilities institution that closed in 2015) had been vacant for ~10 years. After an initial failed auction sale, the property was purchased by Sammatin tila Oy (75%) and Nimetön Oy (25%) for 396,270 € (with 35,000 € compensation for discovered contaminated soil).

The purchase made regional news (Yle, Aamulehti). Suvi Rajala outlined three potential development directions:
1. Solar energy production
2. Data center hosting
3. Daycare facility in the protected manor house

The area spans ~19 hectares on the shores of Näsijärvi, with ~20 buildings (mostly 1960s/70s, some 1920s protected structures).

### 2025 — Food Committee Appointment
Suvi Rajala was elected to Suomen Yrittäjien (Finnish Entrepreneurs) ruokavaliokunta (food committee) for the 2025–2026 term, representing Pirkanmaan Yrittäjät.

### Current Status
The farm operates with:
- ~130 hectares of cultivated land
- Robotic milking of Ayrshire, Holstein-Friesian, Western Finnish cattle, and crossbreeds
- 4 permanent employees + 7-8 seasonal workers
- 1.96 million € annual revenue
- Three rental cabins (Hauenmaa, Matikka, Villakko)
- 24/7 self-service shop
- Ongoing Ylinen development planning
